# :crossed_swords: 《T5C CLAN 》 :crossed_swords:
## :sparkles: ABOUT US :sparkles:
T5C is a Roblox Rivals clan built for players who want a friendly community while staying competitive. We focus on squad play, grinding levels together, and helping each other improve in-game.

## :scroll: CLAN RULES

Respect Everyone: No toxicity, disrespect, or unnecessary drama.

Stay Active: Play regularly and hang out in our Discord channels.

No Cheating: Zero tolerance for hacking, exploiting, or glitching.

Represent Positively: Keep a clean reputation in public servers.

Follow Calls: Listen to team leaders during scrims and competitive matches.

## :clipboard: REQUIREMENTS

:chart_with_upwards_trend: Level: 80+ (100+ preferred)

:knife: Movement: Good mobility and dodging skills

:dart: Aim: Solid mechanics and fast reaction time

:people_hugging: Attitude: Respectful, chill, and open to feedback

## :trophy: UPCOMING T5C TOURNAMENT

・An official clan tournament is coming soon!
・Open to all eligible clan members.
・Compete against other members and fight for the title of **T5C Tournament Champion**.
・Check our Discord announcements for more details!

## :question: WHY JOIN T5C?

Access to private servers for aim, movement practice, and custom matches.

Always have active teammates online to squad up for rank.

Organized clan structure with regular internal events and tournaments.
